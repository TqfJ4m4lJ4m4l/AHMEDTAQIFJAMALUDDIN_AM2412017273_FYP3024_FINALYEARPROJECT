Our wider setting

Psychiatric Faculty

The Psychiatric Faculty brings together services and professionals who support mental health and wellbeing. Neurodivergent people may also seek help with stress, anxiety, sleep, or other concerns, and their support should take their individual communication and sensory needs into account.

Why this service exists

Why This Department?

Neurodivergent people can face barriers when services expect everyone to communicate, learn, or manage daily life in the same way. A dedicated service can help people discuss their needs and find appropriate support.

Support should fit the person

Ask what helps, explain choices clearly, allow time to respond, and include the person in decisions about their care.
